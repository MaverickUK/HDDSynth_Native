About

A drive here is pure synthesis: tones locked to the rotation, a body they pass through, air noise in bands, and an arm whose seeks ring its modes. Recorded seek sounds (grains) are an optional extra. The fitted presets come from the sample packs in HDDSynth_Samples (tools/drive-lab-v2/fit.js); edit any of them, or start from the designed drive.

Part of HDDSynth Native (tools/drive-lab-v2), licensed under the GPLv3. Work in progress: the firmware still runs v1's drive.json.
