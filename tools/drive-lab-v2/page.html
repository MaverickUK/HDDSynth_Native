1. Choose a sample pack

A pack is a folder of recordings of one drive: idle.wav (spinning), access.wav (seeking), spinup.wav and spindown.wav. Only idle.wav is needed; each other one adds a part of the model. Nothing leaves your computer: it all runs in this page. Packs to try are in HDDSynth_Samples.

2. Building the model

    Each step measures the recording, then renders the model and corrects it until the two measure alike.

    3. Listen

    How should it make its seeks?

    Synthesised: the model makes every seek itself, like the rest of the drive, so no two are the same. Recorded: it plays real seeks instead, cut from the pack's access.wav (), picking a short one for a short seek and a long one for a long seek. Mixed: some of each. The spinning is always synthesised.

    This changes the drive live (power it on and choose Random reads) and the Seeking row above. Recorded and Mixed need those snippets, so they come in a second, small file that goes beside drive.json. With Synthesised, drive.json is all the drive needs.

    Or try one fitted already

    These were built from the packs in HDDSynth_Samples by the same code. To compare one with its recording, import that pack.

    Advanced: every number in the model

    A model, not recordings

    A drive here is a description of how it sounds: the tones its motor, bearing and platters make as multiples of the rotation speed, the casing they ring through, the air noise, the arm's resonances, how it seeks, and what its heads do at power on and off. Every sample is made live from that, so no two seeks are alike and it can follow what the PC does.

    The IDE Synth Bay

    The Bay watches the PC's disk activity and tells the HDD Synth: busy or not, random or sequential, and where the head should be. A modelled drive answers each one: the activity panel on the left sends the same kinds of thing.

    Recorded seeks (optional)

    An imported drive can play real seeks instead of, or mixed with, synthesised ones: a couple of dozen short snippets cut from the pack's recording (the code calls them grains), kept in a small WAV beside drive.json. A drive without them is pure synthesis, and needs only drive.json.

    On the HDD Synth
