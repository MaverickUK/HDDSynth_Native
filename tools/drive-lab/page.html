What this is

A design tool for the HDD Synth, a small board that plays the sound of a mechanical hard drive in time with your PC's disk activity. Instead of recordings, a pack can be this model: the HDD Synth's firmware runs the same engine live, with the head moving to the drive's real physics.

Put your drive on an HDD Synth

  1. Save the settings as drive.json (above).
  2. Put it in a new folder in /samples on the HDD Synth's SD card, for example /samples/MyDrive/drive.json.
  3. Choose the pack with the action button. It needs firmware v0.20.0 or later.
